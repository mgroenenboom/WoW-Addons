# WoW Addons

## VendorMark

Mark items in your bags to sell the next time you visit a vendor, then sell them (along with Blizzard's gray junk) with one click.

**Usage**
- **Alt+Left-Click** an item in your bags to mark or unmark it. Marked items get a gold coin icon in the top-right corner and a line on their tooltip.
- When you talk to a merchant, a **Sell Marked + Junk** button appears above your bag window. Click it to sell every marked item plus everything Blizzard already counts as junk.
- Marks are saved per character, by item ID, so every copy of a marked item is sold, even after you move it to another slot.

**Slash commands:** `/vm list`, `/vm clear`, `/vm sell`

**Install:** Copy the `VendorMark` folder into `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`.
